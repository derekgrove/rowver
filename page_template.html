      A note on .rvr compatibility
      Sorry — I made a kind of "foundational error" in my original design: I forgot to
      consider unweighted events (real data, or anyone who simply has no event weights and
      doesn't care to use them). I've made a small change that handles both cases, files
      with and without weights.
      The catch: .rvr files written by this new version will not open in older copies of
      the page. This page reads everything, old and new — but if you are using a page I
      sent you earlier, just ask me for an updated one and I'll give it to you right away.
      Thanks!
      — Derek
